How to get rich in UTM

Like I said, UTS and UTM have similarities but theres mostly noticeable differences. There is server events which can help you in gameplay, such as special customers and money rain, where as in UTS there is no events. UTS starts off with Sam, in UTM you start off as a white character but you are able to customize yourself after getting money.

#1 : Collect plants

If its UTS I would never say anything like this, but in this case UTM is different. Around the map there are some plants that sparkle either yellow, blue, or rainbow. Rainbow being rare, and yellow being more likely to find. collecting plants can earn you over than $100 in game.

#2 : Get a job(seriously)

This will help you alot since there is an event where you get special customers, even though it only lasts 2 mins that should be enough time to grind and earn money quickly.

#3 : Rob and kill npcs

Killing npcs can help you level up(doing anything in the game can help you level up honestly), when you level up you can actually claim money and awards from the leving chart, such as gems and leveling outfits made for UTS characters that can wear when you play do play UTS again.

#4 : Do some of the same tips I said for UTS

I ran out of ideas but you can actually buy money in the game with robux, which is cool. Sadly there is no hidden gem quest in UTM for any item that is a weapon or a gear you can use(not food) costs a amount of money and also requires a certain level to reach to get it.

#5 :Collect chests around the map

Collecting chests can help you since they are obviously chests, they have teasure/money in them. You should look at the snow biome and at the Village Isle, behind the mountian there is a cave where you can find a chest after you kill the two bandits guarding it. DO NOT go through the door next to the maze entrance(which is a hole in the wall) where there is more bandits that will kill you if you try to enter it. In the Village Isle there should be a cave and when you enter there is a maze(again) but dont be fooled because once you find the chest there is actually an upstairs and there is a chest there too. Another chest is by the Isle, there should be a sunken submarine and there is a chest near it, but it is annoying since you have to stay sill to get it and you are constanly swimming and moving prohibiting you from geting the chest's money.
