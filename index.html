#1 The most efficent way:

Collect trash and sell to David/the trashman.

Although looking into trash and dumpsters take time since your walking around the map, but it is the most easiest way to gain more than 100 dollars. Usually the trash can be common all the way to impossible loots. Common having a little amount and godly having a tremendous amount of trash. You can sell the trash by Sam's house.

#2 Become a bounty hunter.

It is hard since you have to gain a couple of dollars to buy a radar and rope, including a weapon you buy from the gun store. That will be easy because you can collect and sell trash. You can choose to do a easy hunt first and gain 100$ or more depending if your taget is brought into jail dead or alive.
