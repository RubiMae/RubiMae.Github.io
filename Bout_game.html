Information of UTS+UTM

This is the map of UTS, basically called the town of Vitex. There is another smaller island called the Village Isle that is not included in the picture above.


In the game there is a thing called the hidden gem quest. I will help you complete it."What is this quest?" This is a quest that the objection is to collect 5 gems that represent colors of the rainbow to get powerful weapons and items. "Why should I complete this?" This quest is entirely optional but the results can help you do tasks more easier.

#1: Red gem

How to get the red gem is to head to the burger joint and grab the bottom bun and put as much red toppings on it as possible. The next thing to do is give that so called "burger product" to Pat/the guy behind the register in the Pizza Place.

you have to do this 7 more times to get the red gem. After, head behind the trading post in the snow biome. There should be a off-color entrance that leads you into a cave. Find the totem with the red mark and place it there.

this will give you a magnet with can auto loot enemies/npcs, but you have to kill them first.


#2 :Blue Gem

How to get this is bring a shovel(look at from side of vitex where the snow biome and the beach touch.) and bring it to the village Isle(smaller island than Vitex) and find the big red x at the back of it and dig it until you get a bottle. Go back and give it to Bob/the guy behind the register in the burger joint. He should give you the blue gem as an award. Go back to the cave where you place your red gem and do the same with the blue gem.

This should give you a laser riffle. It takes a few seconds to kill npcs though its better and faster than some guns you have to buy.

#3 : Purple Gem

All you have to do is give ten medicine bottles to David the trashman. There is two options, its either buy ten medicine bottles from the hospital or go back and forth giving medicine from the storage room to him. I would say buying ten medicines is faster, but if you wanna be cheap... Do the second one. Each bottle is $14.99, ten of them would be $149.90.

This will give you the demon slayer sword, to be honest I would rather use the guns for they can kill more faster and is long range.

#4 : Yellow Gem

You have to give 150 golden berries to the shopkeeper in the Isle Village, meaning you have to grind to get goldenberries which are located at the mayor's mansion around three times getting 53 berries each stop. The berries won't respawn after you pluch them, so you have to leave the game and join back so they can appear again.

This will give you golden shears which will harvest plants 4x more faster.

#5 : Green Gem

This is kinda more hard but since you can grind for money it will be more easier, to get this final give is to go to the gas station and by it for 3344.99 dollars. Once you done it, it will give you a megaphone where you can scream at npcs by intereacting with them.

Place all of them together in the cave.

Once you done this all the gems will glow and connect together creating a bigger rainbow gem. This will active the last two items you are able to collect before you get the badge for completing the hidden gem quest. These two items are a spray paint bottle which allows you to draw around the map, and a rainbow lazer minigun.
